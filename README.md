## Overview
*Sarah Kim creates a new identity for herself with carefully constructed lies; then a body is found and identified as hers; gradually, the investigation uncovers the truth, while Sarah's double life crumbles.*
## 📖 The Great Plot: The Con Artist
The narrative follows **Sarah Kim** (Shin Hae-sun), a woman born into nothing who decides to rewrite her destiny by weaving a flawless web of lies.
1. **The Ghost Identity:** She leaves her impoverished life behind and builds an entirely fraudulent persona as the elite luxury executive behind an elusive, fake high-end label called *Boudoir*.
2. **The Complication:** Her perfectly manicured empire faces total destruction when a decomposing body is discovered in a Seoul sewer grate—carrying an ID card with her real birth name.
3. **The Game of Cat-and-Mouse:** Enter Detective Park Mu-gyeong (Lee Jun-hyuk). As he digs into the murder, he faces Sarah's labyrinth of aliases, falsified bank records, and fabricated corporate histories. Every time the police think they have her cornered, Sarah stays three steps ahead.
 
## ⚖️ The Climax: How She Won the Courtroom
The series culminates in a thrilling, multi-episode legal war where Sarah faces charges of massive financial fraud, identity theft, and complicity in murder.
 
Instead of breaking down, Sarah treats the courtroom as her final stage:
* **Weaponizing Her Deceptions:** Sarah uncovers systemic corruption within the elite class that she conned. She systematically exposes how her wealthy "victims" used her fake luxury label, *Boudoir*, to launder millions in dirty money and evade taxes.
* **The Ultimate Flip:** By shifting the narrative, she transforms herself from a malicious scammer into a whistleblowing anti-hero in the eyes of the public and the jury.
* **The Verdict:** Her legal defense team skillfully argues that the financial elites willingly participated in her schemes out of pure greed, invalidating their status as victims. Exploiting these legal loopholes, Sarah walks out of the courthouse completely acquitted of the heaviest charges—cementing her status as the ultimate con artist who beat the system at its own game.
